As a user or agent, I ask the `extras-autonomous-work` skill to take one or more work items to completion without me answering along the way. After confirming the set of items and their order once at the start, the agent drives each item through the `commands-drive-work-to-completion` skill, one after another. Wherever a stage would ask me — an open question, a review, a qa verdict — it gives the same self-contained question to read-only advisors, weighs their answers as arguments rather than votes, and decides itself.

Which advisors, how work is reviewed and tested without a person, and when a version is cut come from my project's `unattended-work` procedure, read with `tcw work procedure unattended-work`. With nothing configured, that is TCW's default: two advisors, Codex and a separate read-only subagent on the strongest model the harness can start, and it never cuts a version or publishes anything. My project replaces it under `work.procedures.unattended-work`.

Whatever my project configures, it stops and reports only on a hard blocker: credentials or another step only a person can take, anything that cannot be undone, a question about what the product should be, spending on my paid accounts, advisors who disagree about a choice that cannot be undone, or a spec that contradicts the code. It records each question, what each advisor said and what it chose in the round it writes next for that stage, so the run can be reviewed afterwards. Where no round is written, because a person decides the qa verdict or the backend keeps qa, those decisions go in the findings comment it posts on the item, or in its report when it posts nothing.
