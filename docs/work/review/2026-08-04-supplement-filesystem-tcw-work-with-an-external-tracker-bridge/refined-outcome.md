## Accepted deviations

- **Criterion 2 is partly met.** The criterion reads as if two claimants can never
  both claim a ticket. Goal 1 was restated on 2026-09-12 in conditional form: "when
  the tracker's workflow can express that". The verifier confirmed three things:
  - on a directed workflow, the second claimant is refused;
  - on a workflow that offers the claim from every status, both claims succeed;
  - two runs by the same account at the same moment can both create an item.

  The last two are the documented limits in `work/manage-external-tracker-intake`,
  and strict mode refuses the every-status case. Accepted as meeting goal 1.
- **Criterion 1's "no test edited" does not strictly hold.** Commit `59162aa5`, from
  an earlier child, changed `tests/test_documented_cli_surface.py` to list the
  `tracker` command. That was disclosed in its commit message. Accepted.
- **A stale capability sentence was fixed during verification** (`40d3f03e`).
  `work/inspect-external-tracker-work` had still called lifecycle sync and strict
  mode "not yet built".
