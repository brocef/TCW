# v2.6.1

## Importing a ticket no longer marks it In Progress

- **`tcw work tracker import` and `tcw work inbox accept` now leave the ticket in
  the backlog, like the item they create.** The claim still moves the ticket
  first, because that is what stops two people taking the same ticket at once.
  Once the item exists, TCW moves the ticket back to where it was before the
  claim. It stays assigned to you. A ticket taken out of triage goes back to your
  backlog status, not to triage.
- **Starting the item is what moves the ticket to In Progress now.** If your team
  read "In Progress" in Jira as "someone has picked this up", look at the assignee
  instead.
- **Your workflow needs a way back from the active status.** Where it offers no
  single transition back, the import still works and prints a warning saying where
  the ticket stayed. Move it yourself, or leave it until you start the item.
- **Unchanged under strict mode,** and for a ticket you already held and were
  working on when you imported it.
- **One narrow gap.** While the ticket is back in the backlog it can be claimed
  again. Two people importing the same unassigned ticket within the same few
  seconds could both end up with an item. Anyone later is refused, because the
  ticket is assigned to you.
